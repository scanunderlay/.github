# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve Scan Underlay's software. Our repositories are developed by the Scan Underlay team, so we don't accept pull requests from outside the company. A pull request from outside is closed without review.

You can still help:

- **Found a bug or have an idea?** Open an issue in the repository. The issue forms ask for what we need to act on it.
- **Found a security problem?** Never open an issue. Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
- **Anything else, or anything sensitive?** Email us. [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md) lists which address to use.

Everyone who takes part follows our [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
